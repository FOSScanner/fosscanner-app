FOSScanner is a privacy-first, free and open-source document scanner.

Scan documents with your camera, and FOSScanner automatically detects the
page's edges, lets you fine-tune the corners with a draggable overlay, and
corrects the perspective (dewarping) into a flat, upright page — all
on-device, using real OpenCV image processing.

Features:

* Automatic edge detection with manual corner adjustment
* Scan filters: Original, Auto-Enhance, Grayscale, and Black &amp; White,
  with live previews before you confirm
* Re-edit a page's corners or filter after the fact without re-scanning
* Capture multiple pages and combine them into a single PDF
* Share the PDF via the OS share sheet
* Material 3 UI that follows your system's light/dark theme
* No accounts, no cloud storage, no tracking

Privacy:

* All image processing and PDF generation happens on-device
* Imported gallery originals are never modified or deleted. App-owned camera temp files
  are removed after the app attempts to copy them into memory, including failed reads
* An unfinished draft is saved automatically in the app's private, OS-managed cache so it
  can be restored after an app restart. Clearing the document removes it; after sharing, you
  choose whether to clear or keep the draft. The OS may evict cached data, and sharing may
  create a separate cached PDF copy
* The app makes no network requests of its own
